Who we are

We started on the operations side, not the marketing side.

OmniCompost was founded by a product engineer and a former waste-logistics manager who spent years watching commercial kitchens pay twice — once for the food, once to landfill it. The consumer digester proved the biology at household scale. The commercial line scales that same closed-loop system to a site that produces hundreds of pounds of organic waste a week.

Our discipline is measurement. Every claim on this page is a figure we can reproduce on a site walk, and every system ships with diversion reporting your compliance officer can file. Where a capability is still in pilot, we label it as a pilot, so a procurement team can trust every figure on this page without a site visit to check it.

What that means for your operation

Compliance, handled

SB 1383 organic-waste diversion with monthly tonnage reporting, audit trail, and signage included — no separate consultant required.

A line item that shrinks

On-site processing cuts organic-waste hauling frequency. Most sites see a measurable drop in pickups within the first quarter.

Engineered to throughput

Modular units sized to your covers or headcount — from a 200-seat restaurant to a multi-building campus — with serviced uptime.

Outputs you can use

Finished compost and liquid feed for on-site landscaping, or scheduled offtake. The loop closes on your property, not a county facility.
