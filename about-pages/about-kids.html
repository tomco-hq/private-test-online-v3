🏡 Once upon a kitchen…

A long time ago (okay, a few years ago) there was an inventor who LOVED worms. Like, really loved them. They watched us munch up old apple cores and turn them into magic dirt that makes plants grow SUPER tall. “Whoa!” they said. “Everybody needs a worm buddy!”

So they built us a cozy worm house with comfy floors and a little tap at the bottom for worm juice (it’s good for plants, I promise — not for drinking, silly!). And that’s how Wormtown was born! 🥳
