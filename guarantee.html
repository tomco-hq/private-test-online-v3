A first season, without the risk.

The guarantee.

A digester is a long acquaintance, and a worm colony takes a few weeks to find its feet. So the guarantee comes in two parts — one for the object, one for the living half of it.

I.

Thirty days for the hardware.

Return the digester or any tool within thirty days of delivery for a full refund of its price. Live the season's first weeks with it on the counter — if it isn't right for the kitchen or the household, send it back.

  • Thirty days from the delivery date, on the digester, the Mini, the bundle, and every garden tool.
  • Refunded in full to the original payment. Across Orange County we collect it; elsewhere a prepaid return label is sent.
  • Returned in usable condition — the original crate kept where it can be. Ordinary marks of a trial run are expected, not penalized.
II.

A living guarantee on the worms.

Worms are shipped live in moist bedding, and a colony settles at its own pace — eating lightly for the first two or three weeks before it finds its rhythm. The living half of the order is covered for that establishment, not just for arrival.

  • Arrive other than alive and healthy — replaced at no charge, no return required.
  • Fail to establish within the first six weeks of ordinary feeding — a fresh pound is sent, once, on the original order.
  • A short note on the bin's conditions helps the next batch settle; the troubleshooting card and the field guide cover the common causes.

The two windows run in parallel: the thirty-day hardware return and the six-week living guarantee both start the day the order arrives.

III.

To start a return.

Write to hello@omnicompost.example with the order and a line on what went wrong. A refund or a replacement is arranged the same week — no queue, no restocking fee.

Start the loop.

Thirty days to return the hardware, six weeks of cover on the worms, and a season to see what the garden does with it.
