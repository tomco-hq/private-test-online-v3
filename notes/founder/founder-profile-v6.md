## How to read this profile

Each claim splits what was **observed** (checkable in the repo) from what is **inferred**
(interpretation you can disagree with without doubting the facts). Every claim carries a
**confidence** level and a **source**. **Sampling caveat:** drawn from one project's files;
it describes the author *in this repo*, filtered through a curated artifact.

## What v6 adds — the self-analysis method

v5 read five invented founders for what stayed constant (the invariance method). v6 watches
what happens when the author is asked to **analyze and then extend their own cast**: a
demographic/market meta-analysis, an inferred media/diet/social read, a stay-the-course-vs-branch
strategy memo, and finally two new founders (v9, v10) built specifically to fill the gaps the
analysis surfaced. The new signal is **second-order**: not just what the author writes, but how
they reason about their own writing and what they change — and pointedly don't — when handed the
freedom to diversify.

## Thesis

The v5 thesis holds and sharpens. The author runs four deliberately-opposed audience registers
over a *single, involuntary* value system — measured honesty, under-promise, the solo maker who
controls the whole chain. **What v6 adds:** when the author analyzes that invariance in their own
work and is then explicitly invited to break it (fill demographic gaps), they **preserve the
creed verbatim and vary only the surface** — ethnicity, age, trade, region. The instinct to hold
the temperament constant is so strong it survives even a direct prompt to diversify. The range is
in register and now demonstrably in *demographic surface*; the person underneath still does not vary.

_Falsifier: if, asked to add an Asian-American maker and an elder maker, the author had given them
genuinely different values — a hype-driven scaler, someone indifferent to disclosure — the
"single involuntary ethos" claim would weaken. They did not. v9 and v10 carry the same five
numbers, the same under-promise line, the same lone-maker origin as the original eight._

## Tensions they're aware of

- **Wide register range, narrow character range** _(from v5, now strengthened)._ The author
  code-switches four audience voices flawlessly, yet every person they invent — across biography
  *and now across ethnicity and age* — is the same restrained solo maker who distrusts marketing.
  v9 and v10 prove diversity of surface without diversity of soul. _(Inferred; raised to
  Medium–High — the sameness now survives an explicit diversify prompt, which a brand-cohesion
  explanation alone doesn't fully cover.)_
- **Representation-as-set-dressing risk** _(NEW; sharpened, then tested)._ Filling demographic gaps
  by holding the template fixed and swapping the face risks reading as interchangeable diversity.
  The risk is **not only the photo** — it's the voice. A staged focus group (Panel 9 in
  `mockups/profiles/FOCUS-GROUPS.md`) framed this as "ten faces, one script," but reading the actual
  v9/v10 HTML *narrows and sharpens* the finding rather than confirming it: the author had in fact
  already individuated most of each founder's register — Linh reasons in clay, glaze, and aging; Hal
  in tolerances, wear, and "a part either holds or it doesn't." The genuine tell was small and
  precise — a few load-bearing lines left verbatim across founders, one of which (a bench caption,
  "they age the way I want the bin to") wore the *ceramicist's* worldview in the *machinist's* mouth.
  Those lines were then corrected in v9/v10, each caption rewritten into its own trade's logic.
  Reading: even the author's involuntary invariance instinct mostly stops at temperament and the
  numbers — it does **not** flatten voice as completely as a first pass suggested; the leakage is at
  the level of a few shared sentences, not the whole script. The shared lines that remain by design
  (spec numbers, the closing honesty pledge) are the invariant *promise*, an asset, not the
  liability. The dossier is reconciled to this distinction. _(Observed flag, then partially
  self-corrected in the work; Medium.)_
- **Polishes past done** _(carried)._ The analytic energy this session went to studying and
  extending the founder cast — already a strong surface — while the standing backend gap
  (checkout) and the synthetic-headshot gate remain open. _(Inferred; Medium.)_
- **Front-of-house leads back-of-house — by budget, not blind spot** _(carried; High)._

## Held loosely (low confidence — guesses, not evidence)

- **Media / diet / social taste** — still essentially unsupported. The author's own dossier marks
  it LOW and notes it describes the target customer as much as the writer. The one inference with
  even medium footing is **restraint**: a low-volume, high-craft, proof-over-hype disposition that
  five-then-ten invented makers couldn't stop repeating. _(Low, except restraint: Medium.)_
- **Coastal-California identity** — inferred from place-names; now ten-for-ten coastal SoCal makers,
  *mild* evidence it's native, still weak. _(Low.)_
- **Won't pass off the synthetic as real** — the headshot gate held again: v9/v10 ship a labelled
  portrait *placeholder*, never a fabricated face. Consistent with the honesty trait; could be
  ordinary risk-aversion. _(Low–Medium.)_

## In one line

Handed their own cast to analyze and a direct invitation to diversify it, the author audits
themselves with the same honest instrument they use on the product, then writes two new founders
of different ethnicity and age who say exactly what the first eight said — proof the creed isn't a
costume but the temperament underneath every mask they make.
