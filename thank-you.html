The order is in.

Thank you.

Your digester is on its way, and the season starts soon. A confirmation is headed to your inbox with the order and the week to expect it.

I.

What happens next.

  • A confirmation, shortly.

    The order number and the delivery week, by email. Keep it for the guarantee dates — both windows start the day the order arrives.

  • The worms, timed separately.

    Live worms ship on their own schedule, set to land the same week as the digester rather than sit waiting in a box.

  • Setup, across Orange County.

    Within the county, delivery and the first placement are complimentary. Elsewhere, the printed field guide in the box walks you through the first hour.

The first weeks.

A new colony settles before it works. For the first two to three weeks the worms eat lightly and little seems to happen — the bin is establishing, not failing. The living guarantee runs through the first six weeks for exactly this reason.

Keeping it going

In time the season asks for a little more — bedding to refresh the trays, a top-up of worms, a spare washer for the tap. A seasonal Tending Kit gathers them with a Field Notes card, edition by edition. Join Field Notes and we'll write when the next one is ready.

II.

The bench grows by word of mouth.

If someone you know is ready to start the loop, send them on. They'll begin their first season with a welcome from us, and we'll send a season's bedding to your door by way of thanks. Reply to your confirmation with a name, or write to hello@omnicompost.example.
